The 3×3 cube

43,252,003,274,489,856,000 positions. Exactly one of them is solved.

This cube turns like a real one. Drag a face to turn that layer, or drag the space around it to look at another side. Hold your own cube with yellow on top and green facing you and it will match the screen.

Turn by name

Every turn has a plain name. Press one, watch the layer move, then do the same on your cube.

Back turns are described as you see them from the front, so you never have to flip the cube around to check.

Never more than 20 turns away

Every one of those positions can be solved in 20 turns or fewer, counting a half turn as one. Tomas Rokicki, Herbert Kociemba, Morley Davidson and John Dethridge proved it in 2010 with about 35 years of computer time donated by Google.

      Half a century of turning

      1. 1974

        Ernő Rubik, a design teacher in Budapest, builds a working model to show how separate blocks could turn around a shared core without falling apart.

      2. 1977

        It goes on sale in Hungarian toy shops as the Bűvös kocka, the Magic Cube.

      3. 1980

        Ideal Toy takes it worldwide and renames it the Rubik's Cube.

      4. 1982

        The first world championship is held in Budapest. Minh Thai wins with a solve of 22.95 seconds.

      5. 2004

        The World Cube Association forms to run official competitions with shared rules, like the 15-second inspection and the stackmat timer.

      6. 2010

        A computer search proves that 20 turns is enough for every position.

      7. Today

        The fastest solvers finish in under four seconds.
