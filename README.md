# Super One

One offline app with 29 apps inside.
- **Everyday:** Calendar, Notes, Reminders, Clock (world clock, alarms, stopwatch, timer), News (saved for offline), Map (world map + GPS), Weather (saved for offline).
- **Create:** Photos, Videos (save from a direct video link), Music (your songs, built-in tunes, piano, beat maker), Voice Memos, Studio (draw, edit photos, filters), Files.
- **Learn:** CFOP Learner (Rubik's cube in plain words), Flashcards, Learn (math, periodic table, planets, quizzes), Dictionary (87,000 words), Translate (offline phrasebook + live translate), Bible (3 public-domain translations), Wikipedia (save articles for offline). CFOP Learner covers all 119 cases (41 F2L, 57 OLL, 21 PLL).
- **Play & social:** Games (10 games + links to all your own games), Chess, Comics (read .cbz comics, make your own), Spin Wheel, Social (opens your social apps).
- **Tools:** Calculator, Measure (ruler, level, converter, sound meter), Links (your favorite websites), Passwords (encrypted vault).

Everything you make is stored only on your own device. Open the page once with internet, then add it to your Home Screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). After that it works with no internet. Weather, News, live Translate and video links use the internet only when you use them.
